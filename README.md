## Ecommerce_Olist_Store_Project
The Olist Store E‑commerce Project is a popular dataset and case study used in analytics and data science to explore how online marketplaces operate. Olist is a Brazilian e‑commerce platform that connects small businesses to major online marketplaces, enabling them to sell products nationwide.

### 📝 Problem Statement
The Olist e-commerce dataset contains detailed information about customer orders, payments, reviews, and deliveries.
The challenge is to analyze this data to uncover customer behavior patterns, payment preferences, and logistics performance.
Specifically, the project focuses on answering these business questions:
- How do customer purchase behaviors differ between weekdays and weekends? 
- Which payment methods are most associated with highly satisfied customers (review score = 5)? 
- How long does it take for pet shop orders to be delivered? 
- What are the spending patterns of customers in São Paulo? 
- How does delivery speed impact customer satisfaction? 

### Dataset and Data Dictionary
The Olist dataset comprises nine separate datasets, all of which are stored in CSV format:

1. olist_order_items_dataset
2. olist_orders_dataset
3. olist_order_payments_dataset
4. olist_order_reviews_dataset
5. olist_products_dataset
6. olist_customers_dataset
7. olist_sellers_dataset
8. olist_geolocation_dataset
9. olist_category_dataset<br>

### 🔎 Data Insights
- 🗓️ **Weekday vs Weekend Sales:** 77% of payments occur on weekdays → opportunity for weekend promotions  
- 💳 **Payment Preferences:** Credit cards dominate 5-star reviews → digital payments drive satisfaction  
- 🚚 **Delivery Performance:** Faster shipping improves customer review scores 
- 🐾 **Category Insight:** Pet shop orders take ~11 days on average → logistics optimization needed  
- 📍 **Regional Insight:** São Paulo customers spend more than product price → multi-item purchases common
